Table~\ref{tab:leave-one-study-out} recomputes the main discounted pooling under the following rule. For each effect, omit the primary study whose removal yields the smallest remaining aggregated belief under published-analogue masses and the Evidence Factory-compatible selector. Ties break toward the omitted study with the lower Study ID. If that remaining belief is not strictly lower than the full-sample belief, every candidate omission is reported. Intensities marked with \textsuperscript{*} change under that omission; parentheses after the omitted study give its evidence models for that effect. Storage size is unchanged when S6 (the largest contributor, with 17 of 62 storage-size evidence models) is removed, whereas inference latency drops from belief $1.00$ to $0.75$ when S6 (the largest contributor, with 17 of 51 latency evidence models) is omitted, while retaining intensity \{P, SP\}. Accuracy's largest drop ($0.91$ to $0.78$) arises from omitting S4 (the largest contributor, with 11 of 41 accuracy evidence models). Part of accuracy's pooled belief therefore reflects configuration multiplicity within a single study, consistent with its decrease to 83\% under the mass-preserving split (Table~\ref{tab:sensitivity-mass-preserving}). GPU energy increases under every omission (S3 (+0.21), S13 (+0.20), S14 (+0.16)).
