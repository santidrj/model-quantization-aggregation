Table~\ref{tab:leave-one-study-out} recomputes the main discounted pooling under the following rule. For each effect, omit the primary study whose removal yields the smallest remaining aggregated belief under published-analogue masses and the Evidence Factory-compatible selector. Ties break toward the omitted study with the lower Study ID. If that remaining belief is not strictly lower than the full-sample belief, every candidate omission is reported. Intensities marked with \textsuperscript{*} change under that omission. Storage size is unchanged when the largest contributing study (S6, 17 evidence models) is removed, whereas inference latency drops from belief $0.80$ to $0.49$ under the same omission while retaining intensity SP. Accuracy's largest drop arises from omitting S11 (one evidence model), illustrating that high belief is not explained solely by configuration multiplicity. GPU energy increases under every omission (S3 (+0.14), S13 (+0.14), S14 (+0.12)).
