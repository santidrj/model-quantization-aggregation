# Local Dempster–Shafer combination for belief assignment

Evidence Factory cannot assign custom simple-support masses—undiscounted unsplit \(B\), mass-preserving root \(1-(1-B)^{1/N}\), or equitable \(B/N\)—without sample-size and variability discounts, so belief-assignment synthesis is computed locally. Combination follows Santos (2016) SSM: Dempster's rule applied pairwise on the intensity frame \(\{SN, NE, WN, IF, WP, PO, SP\}\), renormalized after each step, in Evidence Factory aggregation-turn order (`data/processed/evidence-factory-aggregation-order.txt`), with masses left unrounded. Reported conflict is that last pairwise \(K\). Hypothesis selection uses focal adjacent compounds and drops a compound when a contained singleton holds \(\ge 75\%\) of its belief (thesis §5.3.2); non-focal compounds are not candidates. The published analogue matches Evidence Factory aggregated evidence 329074 on intensity, integer-percent belief, last-step \(K\), and evidence-model count.
