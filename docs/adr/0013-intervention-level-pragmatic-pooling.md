# Keep intervention-level Dempster–Shafer pooling as the primary estimator

Review pressure to make the primary study the Dempster–Shafer source (collapse configurations within a publication, then combine across studies) is rejected. The published primary estimator remains discounted combination of evidence models. That choice is a pragmatic pooling of intervention-level support, not a claim that same-publication configurations are independent BPAs. Aggregated belief is therefore glossed as accumulated configuration-level support.

Dependence is addressed without changing the primary mass assignment: the lead sensitivity check stays the undiscounted mass-preserving root (ADR 0010). Theory-diagram intensities use intensity reconciliation with that check (intersection when selected intensities differ; belief-only drops stay in Threats). Results prose continues to lead with the primary estimator.

**Considered options.** Study-level primary BPAs; making mass-preserving or equitable the main estimator; publishing a discounted mass-preserving companion as the lead dependence check (superseding ADR 0010). The last was reopened in response to review and again rejected: ADR 0010’s corpus evidence still holds.
