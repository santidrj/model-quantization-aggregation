# Separate literature and Evidence Factory hypothesis selection

Santos (2015, §5.3.2 and Figure 16) recursively selects direction-compatible intensity intervals from their belief measures and does not require direct mass on the selected interval. Evidence Factory instead reproduces the published synthesis only when selection is restricted to singleton and focal adjacent-compound candidates. We retain Evidence Factory-compatible selection as the default for existing callers and the reproduction gate, while requiring explicit Santos selection for the four belief-assignment comparisons and audit traces. Keeping both policies makes the literature/compatibility disagreement visible instead of silently changing published parity or misattributing Evidence Factory behavior to Santos.
